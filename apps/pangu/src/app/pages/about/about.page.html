About


Pangu (Tamil: பங்கு; English: Share) is a privacy focused open source personal stock portfolio management application for Indian markets.


Features

  • Dashboard with market overview
  • Portfolio tracking with transactions, import and export
  • Stock search with quotes and company details
  • Indices with constituents and charts
  • IPO calendar with issue details, subscription and objectives
  • Watchlists for stocks you follow
  • Glossary of Indian stock market terms
  • Light and dark themes with offline-friendly installable app

Privacy

Your portfolio holdings, transactions, watchlists and settings are stored only on your device — all personal data stays local. No account or sign-up is needed to use Pangu. See our Privacy Policy for details.


Open Source

Pangu is free and open source software built by Hyper Weavers. The source code is available at github.com/hyperweavers/nidhi, where you can also report issues and contribute.
